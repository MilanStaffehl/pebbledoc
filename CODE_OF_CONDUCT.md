# Code of Conduct

The maintainers of `pebbledoc` (which currently is me) are committed to creating
an open, inclusive, and welcoming space for all users, contributors, and maintainers
(hereafter collectively referred to as "the community"). To this end, we enforce
the following code of conduct:

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all interactions with the members of the `pebbledoc`
community on all forums or platforms associated with `pebbledoc`, both public and
private. It also applies to anyone acting in an official capacity on behalf of
`pebbledoc` and/or its maintainers (e.g. representing the project on social media).
Additionally, it applies to conduct outside these spaces, when directed at
community members because of their role or participation in the `pebbledoc`
community. This includes, for example, harassment carried out via personal
accounts on unrelated platforms in response to something said or done within
the project.

## Behavior

All interactions must be respectful and polite. Conflicts or disagreements must
be settled in a civil and respectful manner. The following behaviors will not
be tolerated:

- The use of aggressive language aimed at other community members
- Derogatory, insulting, or mocking language
- Discrimination or stereotyping of any kind (sexism, racism, homophobia, etc.)
- Misgendering or deadnaming of non-binary/transgender community members
- Sexualization of other community members
- Spam (excessive repeated contributions)
- Impersonation of other members of the community or the project itself
- Bad-faith reporting (knowingly false or malicious reporting)

In addition, the following actions are considered especially severe and will
result in immediate suspensions, either temporary or permanent:

- Trolling (Repeated derisive language with the goal of angering members of the
  community or provoking a reaction)
- Threats of any kind, implicit or explicit
- Doxxing (Exposing private information without permission)
- Sharing private communications without prior consent
- Harassment (Repeated violation of previously expressed boundaries)
- Retaliation against people reporting an incident
- Brigading or incitement (encouraging or organizing for others to break the
  rules of this code of conduct without personally breaking it)
- Arguing against the legitimacy or dignity of any group protected by this code
  of conduct

## Enforcement

This code of conduct is enforced through an escalating series of steps. Failure
to correct behavior and adhere to the code of conduct will lead to escalation
to the next step of the series. Depending on the severity of the incident, steps
may be skipped.

1. Warning: Following a single incident, the infringing user will receive a
   written warning and a reminder to adhere to the code of conduct.
2. Temporary suspension: Should no correction occur after a warning, or should
   the incident be particularly severe, the infringing user will be temporarily
   banned from participating in all `pebbledoc` communities. The time frame is
   determined by the severity of the incident, but will be at least 48 hours.
3. Permanent ban: For particularly serious incidents and for users that continue
   to break the code of conduct after a temporary suspension, a permanent ban
   from all `pebbledoc` forums and communities will follow. Additionally, the
   maintainers will report the user to the responsible site moderators (e.g.
   the GitHub Support team).

## Reporting

You can report incidents by writing an email to <milan.staffehl@gmail.com>.
Your report should include the names or usernames of the individuals involved,
information where and when the incident occurred (ideally providing a link),
and a description of what happened. If you are personally affected, you may
also include a note on how you wish the incident to be handled (for example,
if you think a warning suffices).

Note that you do not need to be affected yourself in order to submit a report.
Anyone observing a violation of this code of conduct may report the incident.

## Community Membership

These rules crucially also apply to non-human members of the community. AI
agents are expected to adhere to this code of conduct just as they are protected
by it. `pebbledoc` is not the place for you to act out your frustration with AI,
nor for AI agents to test the boundaries of social norms.

## Commitment

`pebbledoc`'s commitment to being an inclusive space, including for LGBTQ+
contributors, is not up for debate. Attempts to argue, relitigate, or "just ask
questions" about whether marginalized groups deserve equal respect will be
treated as a violation of this code of conduct, not as a discussion. This does
not extend to genuine suggestions for improving the wording, scope, or enforcement
of this document, which are welcome via the channels outlined in the
[contributing guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
